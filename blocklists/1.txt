# phonym blocklist 1 (frozen; a change is a new file, used by new releases)
#
# One entry per line, lowercase a-z, at least 3 letters; # starts a comment.
# BLOCKLIST RULE: a word of a name is not allowed if it contains an entry and has at
# most one letter besides it. "puta", "putas" and "aputa" are blocked; "baputa" and
# "putaren" are not.
#
# Policy. Listed: sexual and kinky terms, body fluids, excretion and genitals, gore
# and violence, slurs and hate symbols. Not listed: plain pejoratives (bonze,
# idiot), religious swears, ordinary words and names, misread transliterations.
#
# 743 entries, transliterated as a reader would say them (c -> k before a/o/u, s
# before e/i; sch/sh -> s; ph -> f; doubled letters single, long vowels kept where
# a style can spell them) and reduced to what some style can spell.
