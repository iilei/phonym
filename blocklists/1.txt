# lowercase a-z, at least 3 letters; lines starting with # are comments.

# to phonym's letters: c->k, ck->k, sch/sh->s, doubled consonants single.
# Very short fragments (dik, sik, kok, ...) are left out on purpose: they would
# block far more harmless names than they protect.
